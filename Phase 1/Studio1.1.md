Create a movie poster featuring a superweapon space station, the subject of the poster. The space station should be a geometric cube shape and have a laser face, and it should be pointing downward firing a red laser beam into a planet. The laser should form a vertical line indicating the might of the cube, and serve as a way to direct the viewer’s eye towards the movie title and release date. The movie title will be “Cubeworld: The Planet Killer” and the release date will be “Christmas 2026”. The laser beam should also be a bright red, contrasting with the darkness of space around it. The cube itself should be smooth and metallic, while the planet below should be blue and green, resembling Earth. The texture of the cube, as well as its position above the planet, should communicate its threatening and hostile nature to the planet below. On the edges of the poster, add some alien space ships that create implied, diagonal lines also pointing towards the title and release date.
